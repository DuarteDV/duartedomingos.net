Don't Touch The Duck tells you exactly what you have to do: touch the duck.

It's a short, simple mobile game that introduces you to the Duck. It's just there, minding its own business, and you're the one who shows up to ruin its day. It will run and hide from you, so pay attention to where it can hide.

It was the first game I ever built, on my own, with Unity and C#.
