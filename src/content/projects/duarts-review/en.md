Duarts Review is a website about the video games I've played, where I share my opinion on them and introduce them to its users.

I built it as a university project with PHP, HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It has a game catalogue, genre and platform filters, a search box and user accounts.

The project was left unfinished: some content and features are missing. My main goal was the design, so that's the part I focused on most.
